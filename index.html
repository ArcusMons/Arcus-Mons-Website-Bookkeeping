How much does bookkeeping cost?

Monthly bookkeeping starts at $50 a month. What you pay above that depends on three things: how many transactions run through the business, how many accounts have to be reconciled, and how far behind the books are when we start. We give you a flat monthly figure on the first call, in writing, before any work begins.

Do you only work with businesses in Utah?

No. We work with businesses in all 50 states. Bookkeeping does not require us to be in the room, and the whole service is built to run remotely. Utah is where we started, not the limit of who we serve.
