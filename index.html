Straight answers

The questions we always get

Which tier will I be in?

Volume decides it. Up to 500 transactions a month sits comfortably in Starter. Past that, you are in Full Service.

What accounting software do you work in?

We only use QuickBooks online. If you use another software, we can transfer your books over.

Do you file my taxes?

We keep your books tax-ready all year and hand a clean, closed package to your CPA.

Am I locked into a contract?

No, our bookkeeping services are month to month. You can cancel at anytime.
